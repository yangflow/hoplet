# Code of Conduct

Hoplet follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer, [@yangflow](https://github.com/yangflow), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code, and may block contributors who do so repeatedly.
