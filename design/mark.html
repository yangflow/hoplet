Hoplet app icon and mark

The app icon is an extruded lowercase h whose arch drops onto a glowing orb, the hop, on an amber to ember gradient field lit from the top left (form B, signed off by the maintainer on 2026-10-12). It is drawn on the macOS icon grid, an 824 unit rounded square on a 1024 canvas. In the web UI the mark is the same h as a stroke glyph in the on-accent colour on the accent tile, like the icons beside it.
